# Week 3 Insights 

## Key Findings 
    1. A small group of customers contributes a significant portion of total sales, indicating that revenue is concentrated among high-value customers.
    2. Customers ranked at the top consistently generate substantially higher sales than the average customer, making them important targets for retention and loyalty programs.
    3. Several customers have placed only one order, suggesting opportunities for customer engagement and repeat-purchase campaigns.
    4. Orders with sales above the overall average represent high-value transactions and may indicate profitable customer segments. 
    5. Window functions such as `RANK()` and `ROW_NUMBER()` provide an efficient way to analyze customer performance and order patterns. 
    6. Common Table Expressions (CTEs) improve query readability and make complex aggregations easier to manage and reuse. 
    7. Combining JOINs, CTEs, and Window Functions enables the creation of comprehensive customer performance reports, including total sales and customer rankings. 

## Conclusion The analysis demonstrates how SQL can be used to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights. By identifying top customers, low-performing customers, and high-value transactions, organizations can make better decisions related to marketing, customer retention, and revenue growth.
